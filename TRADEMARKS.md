and their logos are trademarks of AddLayer and are not covered by that
license (section 6). This page says how they can be used.

## What you can do without asking

Use the name and the logo to refer to DBine itself. For example:

- list DBine in a directory, an integrations page or a marketplace, with its
  logo on its card;
- write an article, a tutorial, a review or a comparison about DBine;
- say that your product, driver or service works with DBine;
- show DBine in a talk, a video or a screenshot.

Keep the logo as it is (no changes to its shape or colors) and don't make it
the most prominent element of your own product or page.

## What needs a different name and logo

- A fork or a modified version of DBine has to use its own name and logo.
  It may say that it is "based on DBine".
- Don't use "DBine" or "AddLayer" in the name of your product, company or
  domain.
- Don't suggest that AddLayer makes, endorses or supports something it
  doesn't.
